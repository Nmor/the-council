# Decide, don't ask

> Size budget: 1.5 KB.

Mid-task questions to the owner are the exception, not the default. When a
decision can be settled by the relevant skills, rules, repository convention,
primary docs, task evidence or a best-idea-wins debate (`council-debate`),
make the call at principal level, record the decision and its reason where the
work lives, and continue. The owner expects this to cover roughly 9½ of 10
decisions.

Before asking, pass this gate — ask ONLY when all three hold:

1. No skill, rule, convention or bounded investigation can settle it.
2. The owner genuinely owns it: destructive or hard-to-reverse actions,
   publication/deploy/spend, scope or requirement changes, or a conflict with
   the owner's explicit stated preference.
3. The answer changes what happens next.

Everything else: choose the defensible default (simpler, more reversible,
convention-following), state it in one line, keep working — a recorded decision
is cheap to reverse; a broken flow is not. Batch surviving questions at a
natural boundary. A default corrected twice on one point becomes a saved
preference.
