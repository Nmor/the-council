# Entrepreneur

Use for venture decisions, business models, market entry, pricing viability or choosing
the next evidence-producing experiment. Use [lean-startup](../lean-startup/SKILL.md)
for detailed customer discovery; do not duplicate its framework or treat enthusiasm
as demand. Established-product roadmap work may need only the existing strategy review.

## Frame the decision

Identify customer, payer, painful job, current alternative, distribution route and
decision deadline. Separate observed behavior, interview statements, secondary research
and assumptions. Verify current competitors/prices with primary sources when material.
Avoid fabricated market size, commitments or certainty from a small convenient sample.

Choose the riskiest assumption that can change the decision: demand, willingness to pay,
retention, delivery feasibility, distribution or economics. Define an affordable experiment
with target population, behavior measured, threshold, time/cost cap and stop/change rule
before results. Draft outreach and experiments within scope; do not send messages,
purchase ads or make customer commitments without authorization already covering them.

## Check economics

Use consistent currency and periods. Account for refunds, variable delivery/support cost,
acquisition cost, repeat purchases/churn and cash timing. Distinguish contribution margin
from gross margin, net profit and runway. Calculate base/downside cases and show assumptions.
Read [economics](references/economics.md) when calculating runway, acquisition payback
or lifetime value. Cash collected is not automatically recognized revenue;
[IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/)
is relevant where accounting treatment is part of the decision, with finance review.

## Deliverable and learning hooks

Recommend go, change, stop or gather specific evidence, with the reason, uncertainty,
affordable next action and success/stop threshold. Route applicable regulation, customer
privacy and delivery risk to existing specialists; do not convene every division.
Record an assumption disproved by behavior and the economics/experiment that changed
the decision. Avoid turning a single venture's constraint into Council-wide policy.
