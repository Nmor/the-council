# Council Conversation Protocol

> **Size budget: 8 KB** — `token-budget.mjs --check`.

Deep review templates for high-risk architecture, security or delivery decisions, or an explicitly requested full Council review.

## Working procedure

1. Establish the relevant mode and existing evidence; do not repeat completed intake.
2. Use the existing plan and current adaptive workflow. Choose only relevant divisions. Research unanswered material questions; do not require speeches, new intake or approvals already authorized. One justified helper at most; independent review and local analysis are distinct.
3. Read only the corresponding sections below before applying their examples.
4. Verify the result with meaningful positive, negative and failure controls. Record actual outcomes, limitations and next action.
